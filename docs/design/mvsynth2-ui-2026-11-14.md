# M-Vsynth2: the screens (knobs, graphs, themes)

**Design and implementation, 2026-11-14.** Status: built and checked on the host build (every page, every theme);
not yet on the FM-1 (section 8). It replaces the screen part of the port proposal's section 7.4 ("four columns:
label, value, bar"). The page table (7.2) and the buttons (7.1) are unchanged.

The code: `firmware/src/vs_ui.c` (included by `mvsynth2.c`), its tables `firmware/gen/vs_ui_gen.h` from
`tools/gen_ui.py`, the font `assets/fonts/dejavu/`. The test: `tests/scripts/ui_all.txt`.

The arrangement (a graph above, four arc knobs below, label and value under each) follows the published screenshots
of Xfer's SHRUB, which is common to most synth screens. Nothing is taken from SHRUB's firmware: the drawing, colours,
type and widgets are our own (flat rings and a flat cap, no knob artwork).

## 1. One layout for every screen

| Band | y | Holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Header | 0..21 | The section as a chip (`SEL`), the page title, a pip a page of that section, 8 voice cells (held, releasing, idle) |
| Panel | 24..127 | A graph of what the page does, drawn from the patch's values (section 3); the readout of the knob in hand |
| Cells | 130..219 | Four cells of 60 x 90: KNOB 1 to 4, left to right, as on the panel |
| Footer | 222..239 | Bank and slot, the name (WARN when edited), the tempo; or `UNSAVED BANK`, or the transfer's counts |

The cells are in the same place on every page and on HOME, so a hand on KNOB 2 always reads the second cell.

Drawing: gfx.c's canvas is 240 x 64 (`CV_MAX`, in the pool), so the panel goes in two strips of 52 rows, a cell is one
canvas of 60 x 90, header and footer one each. Each band keeps a hash of what it shows and is drawn and sent (one
DMA) only when that changes. The panel redraws at most every 40 ms while a knob turns, every 80 ms for the LFO's dot
and HOME's scope. A page change redraws everything (one `lcd_fill`, then the bands).

## 2. The knob cell

| Part | Geometry (in the cell) | Colour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Track | Ring r 18.5, 3 px, 270 degrees, centre (30, 26) | LINE |
| Value arc | The same ring, lit | THEME (the knob in hand: ACCENT) |
| Cap | Disc r 12.5 | RAISED (in hand: an ACCENT rim) |
| Pointer | Line from r 4.5 to r 11, 2 px, round ends | TEXT |
| Label | Centred, top y 54, `FONT_LAB` (10 px) | DIM |
| Value | Centred, top y 68, `FONT_VAL` (13 px); a long one falls back to `FONT_LAB`, tighter | TEXT (in hand: ACCENT) |

The kind comes from the parameter table (`VS_PARAMS[].kind`), so the YAML needs no new field:

| Kind | For | Drawn as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VALUE | `VS_K_NUM`, `VS_K_TABLE` | Arc from the start to the value |
| CENTRE | `VS_K_SIGNED`; PITCH, FINE, TUNE | Arc from 12 o'clock (`vs_param_centre`) to the value, a tick at the top |
| CHOICE | `VS_K_ENUM` | The ring cut into a segment a choice, the one chosen lit; the pointer at its middle |
| SWITCH | `VS_K_SWITCH` | A 38 x 20 switch, not a knob |
| ROUTE | `VS_K_SRC`, `VS_K_DST` (the CTRL routes) | A link, no ring: the value says it |
| none | An empty column | A dot |

The knob in hand: the parameter turned last (`big_id`), for 1.5 s. FREQ carries a 1.5 px outer arc in ACCENT: the
span the filter envelope's AMOUNT adds (2 knob steps an AMOUNT step).

On the chip: `KNOB_ANG` (a pixel's angle on the sweep), `KNOB_RING` and `KNOB_CAP` (coverages, 4 bits) for a 48 x 48
square; one pass over its 2304 pixels, no trigonometry, no division a pixel. The pointer is a distance-to-segment
line from `UI_SIN`.

## 3. The graphs (main loop only)

| Pages | Graph |
| --- | --- |
| LFO 1, LFO 2 (both pages) | One cycle of the wave at its depth; the engine's running phase as a dot; DEST and SPEED as chips. Dim when off or at depth 0 |
| OSC 1 | Two cycles of the shape, DETUNE's copies drifting behind it, FM bending the phase |
| SUB OSC | The SUB at its octave and level over OSC 1, faint |
| OSC 2 | Its shape at its PITCH against OSC 1's two cycles (NOISE as noise) |
| OSC MIX, OSC COMMON | The mix of the two by MIX, with SYNC and RING; chips for SYNC, RING, M.ENV |
| MOD ENV | Attack and decay, signed by AMOUNT, from the middle line |
| FILTER ENV, AMP ENV | A D S R, the segment of the knob in hand in ACCENT |
| FILTER (both pages) | The type's response at FREQ and RES (`UI_FLT`: a drawing, 22 px an octave), dashed where AMOUNT takes it; SAT as a chip |
| AMP | The level against velocity (GAIN, VEL); PAN on a scale |
| FX SWITCH | The signal path: EFFECT, EQ, DELAY, CHORUS as blocks, lit when in circuit |
| EFFECT | By type: the distortions' transfer curves, DECI's steps, RM's product, COMP's curve, the phasers' notches |
| EQ | TONE's tilt and the peak at FREQ, LEVEL, Q |
| DELAY (both pages) | Echoes, left above and right below, by TYPE, falling by FEEDBK, scaled by DRY/WET, R moved by SPREAD |
| CHORUS (both pages) | The delay time swinging, left and right half a cycle apart |
| VOICE | The voices a note may take; PORTAMENTO's glide |
| UNISON (both pages) | The stack's voices spaced by DETUNE |
| CONTROL (both pages) | The two routes, source to destination with SENS, and the bend's range |
| ARP | Sixteen steps of the pattern by TYPE and RANGE, their length by GATE |

Every graph is a picture of the values, not a measurement of the engine: the engine stays the only source of the
sound. The readout of the knob in hand (its label and value, 22 px) sits at the panel's top left; FX SWITCH and
CONTROL leave it out (the blocks and the routes say it).

## 4. The other screens

- **HOME**: the patch's colour, its name (22 px; WARN and `EDITED` when edited), bank and slot; chips for the play
  mode (POLY n, MONO, LEGATO, UNISON), ARP, and the octave when not 0; a scope of the output (220 frames of L + R
  from a rising zero crossing, taken in `app_audio` when asked, auto-gained up to x16); FREQ, RES, AMOUNT, GAIN; at
  the foot CPU, the voice and bus split, the 3 s peak (WARN at 80 % or a shed voice), the tempo.
- **BANK** (SEQ): nine rows around the cursor, each with its number, its patch colour and its name; the cursor row
  framed; the slot playing marked at the left edge; a scroll bar.
- **SAVE**: the name, `TO U1 004`, what the slot holds; BANK (a choice of 8) and SLOT as knobs.
- **GLOBAL 1/2**: MIDI CH, VOICES, TEMPO and CLOCK (read-only: INT or EXT), the voices in use and the tempo large.
- **GLOBAL 2/2** (new): THEME and COLOUR, with a swatch of every theme. GLO walks VOICE .. CONTROL 2/2, GLOBAL 1/2,
  GLOBAL 2/2, then back to VOICE.

THEME's place; "default" keeps the theme's own. Nothing about the screen is in the patch.

## 6. Type

DejaVu Sans Condensed Bold (Bitstream Vera licence), rendered at build time by `tools/gen_ui.py` into 4-bit coverage
in gfx.c's `felucca_font_t` form: `FONT_LAB` 10 px (+1 tracking), `FONT_VAL` 13 px, `FONT_TTL` 15 px, `FONT_BIG`
22 px, ASCII 32..126. The design's IBM Plex Sans Condensed was not on the build machine; DejaVu Condensed fits the
same columns. Terminus (`FONT_S`, `FONT_L`) stays for the platform's own screens (boot, crash, update).

## 7. Costs

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Flash | `vs_ui_gen.h` 54 KB (fonts 39 KB, knob tables 7 KB, filter curves 7 KB) plus the code; the image was 312 KB of 582 KB |
| RAM | Under 1 KB of statics (a curve's rows, the band hashes, the scope's 220 samples). The canvas is gfx.c's, unchanged |
| Audio ISR | The scope's copy: 220 samples, only while HOME is up and something sounds. Nothing else |
| SPI | A full screen 115 KB (as before); the panel 50 KB (about 33 ms at 12 MHz), at most 12 times a second when animated |
| The sound | Unchanged: the host's WAV for `tests/scripts/ui.txt` is byte for byte the one from before |
